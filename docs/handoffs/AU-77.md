### 2. Sharp ribbon while scrolling the landing page

- The user's words: "it blurs not detailed anymore… it was detailed, i want to keep it that way." They want the fine lines from the hero to stay while scrolling the roadmap.
- Cause: every keyframe after the hero in `RIBBON_KEYFRAMES` has `soft: 1`. In the shader, `uSoft` melts the lines and cuts brightness to 12% (`ribbon-scene.ts`, around line 72 to 78).
- Fix: keep the lines sharp (soft 0 or close to it) on the landing page. Keep body text readable by placing the ribbon on the preview side, not under the text. Use the keyframes' `shift` and `offset`, and dim it if needed. On phones (one column, `RIBBON_KEYFRAMES_NARROW`), keep the lines but dim them enough that text stays readable.
- Check readability with screenshots at desktop (1440) and phone (375) widths, at the hero, mid-roadmap and the closing.
