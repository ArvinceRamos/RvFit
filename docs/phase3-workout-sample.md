# Phase 3 workout sample and coverage report

**PLACEHOLDER CONTENT. Not reviewed by a qualified person. Do not present it as reviewed.**

This is the review gate for P3-2 (updated after your feedback on bodyweight equipment and defaults). It was generated from the real content files in `src/lib/workouts/`, so it shows exactly what the app will resolve.

How to read it:

- "Plan" is sets × reps, or sets × seconds for holds. Rest is shown as text only.
- "Swap options" is how many other exercises that slot can be swapped to (same pattern, allowed by the equipment tier, not already used that day).
- Each split is shown at its two equipment extremes (bodyweight and gym). The 3-day beginner plan also shows dumbbells only, and the 4-day split also shows an intermediate gym plan so you can compare levels.
- Splits for 2, 3 and 4 days are shown at beginner level. Splits for 5 and 6 days are shown at intermediate level, and beginner 5 and 6 day samples are at the end of this file.
- Inside each level the same split is used for every equipment tier. Only the exercises change.
- Equipment tiers include the ones below. Bodyweight = floor, wall, sturdy chair or table, and a pull-up bar. Dumbbells = bodyweight kit plus dumbbells (no bench). Gym = everything.
- Level does not decide machine or free weight. Every user can swap a slot to any exercise their equipment allows (for example a dumbbell move when a machine is not available).

- 94 exercises in 12 movement patterns.
- 30 valid templates resolve: 15 beginner and 15 intermediate (beginners can now choose 2 to 6 days). Invalid combinations (such as 7 days) resolve to nothing.
- Beginner days have 4–5 exercises. Intermediate days have 5–7.

- Every slot has at least one swap option in every template. Some bodyweight slots have exactly one.

## What changed after your feedback (round 2 at the end of this section)

- **Pull-up bar is now part of the bodyweight kit.** Bodyweight vertical pull is now standard calisthenics: pull-up, chin-up, negative pull-up, scapular pull-up, dead hang. Bodyweight rows are inverted rows (under a low bar or sturdy table), feet-elevated and wide-grip. The prone raises, "superman pulldown", "snow angel" and towel curl are gone. Biceps now use the underhand inverted row and the close-grip chin-up. Core gained the hanging knee raise.
- **Level no longer decides machines or free weights.** I dropped the idea of a separate beginner pick. Research shows similar muscle gain from machines and free weights when effort is matched, and beginners can use either. The shared `pick` stays. The first gym chest exercise is now the dumbbell bench press, so the gym defaults are a mix (leg press, Romanian deadlift, dumbbell bench, machine shoulder press, cable row, lat pulldown).
- **Swaps already cover "no machine today".** Swap options include dumbbell and bodyweight moves for gym users. Logging accepts any valid option for the slot, so a user can do a dumbbell version for one workout without changing the saved swap.

## Weak spots

1. **Bodyweight pull-up difficulty.** The default for the bodyweight vertical pull slot is the pull-up, and a true beginner may not manage 8 reps. Easier options are one swap away (negative pull-up, scapular pull-up, dead hang, and inverted rows). Same for push-up and pike push-up. I did not add a level-based default because you said level should not decide the exercise.
2. **Dumbbell-only chest has no bench.** Only floor press and floor fly, and the floor limits the range.
3. **Bodyweight side delts, biceps, triceps and calves have only 2 options each,** so one swap. Bodyweight side-delt work is the weakest: wall lateral press hold and side-lying lateral raise. Bodyweight triceps are chair dip and close-grip push-up.
4. **Bodyweight 5 and 6 day plans repeat a small pool,** for example pull days use 2 inverted-row variants from 3.
5. **No loading for bodyweight lower body.** Progression is harder variants (single-leg bridge, split squat), not weight. The Phase 3 progression prompt adds reps only.
6. **Equipment assumptions need a plain statement in the UI later.** Bodyweight needs a pull-up bar and a sturdy chair or table. The Preferences label still says "Bodyweight only". I have not changed it.
7. **Cues are short technique reminders, not safety advice.** All numbers and cues, including sets, reps, rest and holds, need qualified review before launch.

## Structure decisions I made (tell me if you disagree)

- Slot keys are built as `<day>-<pattern>-<count>`, for example `upper-a-horizontal-push-1`. They are permanent, so do not reorder two slots with the same pattern in one day.
- A seconds exercise uses the level's hold range (beginner 20–40 s, intermediate 30–60 s) in place of the slot's rep range. This lets a user swap between a rep exercise and a hold in the same slot.
- The resolver chooses the exercise at the slot's pick index. If that exercise is already used that day, or the list is short, it takes the first unused one.
- `resolveTemplateByKey` turns a stored key back into a template. P3-6 will need it to validate logs.
- Every dumbbell and gym exercise is marked `loaded` (weight is logged). Every bodyweight exercise is unloaded (no weight field).

## Round 2 changes (your "apply")

- **Default pull is now the negative pull-up,** then the pull-up, then the chin-up. People who can already do pull-ups swap once.
- **Dumbbell-only chest** gained the dumbbell squeeze press and the dumbbell-grip push-up.
- **Bodyweight lower body** gained a ladder: chair Bulgarian split squat, single-leg chair squat, single-leg Romanian deadlift.
- **Bodyweight triceps** gained the bodyweight triceps extension (hands on a bar or sturdy table).
- Accepted as is: two options for bodyweight side delts, biceps and calves, and the small pool in 5 and 6 day bodyweight plans.
- Weak spots 1 to 5 above are now smaller. Weak spot 5 (no loading for bodyweight lower body) is answered by the new harder variants. The Preferences note (weak spot 6) is added in P3-4.
